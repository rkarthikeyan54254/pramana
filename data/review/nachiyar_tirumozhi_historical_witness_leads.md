## 1. New highest-priority target: 1909 `Mudhal Ayiram Moolam`

The Digital Library of India Tamil catalog lists four copies of:

- **Title:** `Mudhal Ayiram Moolam`
- **Attributed catalog author:** Periya Vachaan Pillai / Periya Jeeyar
- **Year:** 1909
- **Press:** Sri Sudarsana Mudrakshara Salai, Kanchipuram
- **Pages:** 599 / 605 / 604 / 599 depending on scan
- **DLI barcodes:**
  - `2040100002512`
  - `2040100030308`
  - `2040100036264`
  - `2040100036265`

Catalog source:
`https://sanskritdocuments.org/scannedbooks/DLI_Catalog/DLID_Tamil_Catalog_1_of_1.html`

### Why this outranks the previously noted 1910 `Mudhal Ayiram Vol - 3`

Nachiyar Tirumozhi is structurally part of the **Mudhal Ayiram / First Thousand**. Independent descriptive sources explicitly list the First Thousand as including Periyazhvar Tirumozhi, Tiruppavai, **Nachiyar Tirumozhi**, Perumal Tirumozhi, Tiruchchanda Viruttam, Tirumalai, Tiruppalliyezhuchchi, Amalanadipiran and Kanninun Siruthambu.

Therefore a complete 1909 book cataloged as `Mudhal Ayiram Moolam` is a more direct bibliographic target for Nachiyar Tirumozhi than the ambiguous 1910 `Vol - 3`.

**Important qualification:** the scan itself has not yet been obtained, so coverage of the disputed loci is not scan-proven. Do not cite the 1909 edition as an active witness until pages are inspected.
