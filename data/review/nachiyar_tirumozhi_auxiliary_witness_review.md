## Methodological consequence

The earlier working hypothesis that Project Madurai/TVA form one textual family while Wikisource/Aradhanam form another is useful as a correlation warning, but it is **too coarse to use as a classification rule**.

JIR behaves locus-by-locus:

- 505: aligns with Wikisource/Aradhanam-style reading;
- 526: mixed;
- 549: mixed;
- 604: aligns with PM/TVA-side reading;
- 630: aligns with PM/TVA/Aradhanam against Wikisource.

Therefore:

1. Never assign a witness to a single family solely from a handful of agreements.
2. Track ancestry/correlation at the **locus** level until editorial genealogy is independently established.
3. Never majority-vote digital witnesses.
4. Historical print evidence remains the preferred adjudicator.
5. Nothing in this artifact authorizes `verified=true`.
