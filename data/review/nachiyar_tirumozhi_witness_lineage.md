# Nachiyar Tirumozhi — Witness Lineage & Numbering Note

**Authority:** UNVERIFIED RESEARCH HYPOTHESIS

This note exists to prevent a dangerous future mistake: treating every agreeing website or
edition as an independent vote. Agreement can come from shared editions, copying, or a common
transcription lineage.

## Key finding

Across all eight manually reviewed disputed units, the Tamil Virtual Academy (TVA) online
Nālāyira text aligns strikingly with Project Madurai at the disputed loci. Wikisource and the
pinned Aradhanam PDF frequently align with each other where that pair differs from PM/TVA.

This suggests two *possible* textual/editorial families:

- **Family hypothesis A:** Project Madurai ↔ Tamil Virtual Academy
- **Family hypothesis B:** Tamil Wikisource ↔ Aradhanam

These are **hypotheses only**. Independence/dependence has not been proven.

## High-value locus

The most important unresolved reading remains unit 101:

- PM / TVA: `ததைத்துக்கொண்டு`
- Wikisource / Aradhanam: `ததர்த்திக்கொண்டு`

This is why simple majority voting would be unsafe: two apparent votes on each side may actually
represent only two source lineages.

## Policy consequence

1. Never majority-vote witnesses without lineage evidence.
2. Preserve readings at the locus level.
3. Track numbering schemes separately from text identity.
4. A historical print can help establish textual ancestry, but age alone does not make it canonical.
5. Nothing in this note authorizes `verified=true`.
