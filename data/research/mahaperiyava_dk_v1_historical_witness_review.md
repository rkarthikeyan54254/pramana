# Mahaperiyava DK Volume 1 — Historical Witness Review v0.03

## Scope

This review compares the ten Deivathin Kural Volume 1 pilot chapters against the
acquired 1957–58/1957–59 `Acharya Swamigalin Upanyasangal` historical witnesses.

The purpose is **claim-level evidence triage**, not verbatim reconstruction and not
automatic authority promotion.

## Authority guardrails

- The Part 1 preface establishes the collection-level shorthand-derived transmission.
- Printed wording is still editorially mediated and is **not assumed verbatim**.
- Same theme does not prove textual dependency.
- `earlier_witness_supported` is a claim-level status, not `primary_source_verified`.
- Restricted source text is not reproduced in this artifact.
- This review records candidates; it does not silently rewrite DK teaching records.

## Front-matter result

Part 1, printed pp. vii–viii (PDF pp. 12–13), signed by Ki. Va. Jagannathan on
22-7-1958, states that `Sri Ananthan` took the Mylapore discourses in shorthand,
typed copies were produced, and the material was arranged into published volumes.

Therefore the relation:

```text
shorthand_rendering_of
dependency_status = established
evidence_level = collection
status = supported
```

is justified.

This **does not** by itself prove that `Sri Ananthan` in the preface is
G. S. Ananthanarayanan; that identity continues to depend on the separate
bibliographic/professional-shorthand evidence.

Part 2's 1974 publisher preface confirms that Parts 1 and 3 had already been
published, identifies the inspected Part 2 as a third edition, and announces a
Part 4 as forthcoming. Part 4 publication must therefore remain unconfirmed until
a separate publication object is located.

No item is promoted to `primary_source_verified`.

The strongest historical-witness candidates for later claim-record promotion are:

- `alaya_vazhipadu`
- `bhakti_seyvathu_etharkaga`

Narrower claim-level support is also present for:

- `advaitam`
- `sakala_mathangalukkum_pothuvana_bhakti`

Any actual authority promotion should be made on the concrete teaching/claim record,
not inferred from this collection-level review.
