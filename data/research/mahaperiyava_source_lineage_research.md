## 1932 Madras chain

Official Kanchi biography: https://kamakoti.org/kamakoti/articles/SageOfKanchi6.html

It records that Mahaperiyava's 1932 Madras discourses were reported every day in *The Hindu* and *Swadesamitran* and identifies three subsequent publications, including `ஸ்ரீ ஜகத்குருவின் உபதேசங்கள்`. Tamil Digital Library independently catalogs a 1933 `ஜகத்குருவின் உபதேசங்கள்` as the Chennai discourses.

**Established:** discourse corpus → newspaper reporting; discourse corpus → named book publications.  
**Not established:** any one DK claim → a specific 1932 report/page.

## Five-part Kamakoti topical series

A 1950 Sri Kamakoti Kosasthanam volume advertises five books together: `நமது வேதங்களும் சாஸ்திரங்களும்`, `நமது ஆலயங்களும் மூர்த்திகளும்`, `நமது அனாதி மதம்`, `நமது தர்மம்`, and `நாற்பது ஸம்ஸ்காரங்களும் கர்மானுஷ்டானங்களும்`.

TDL separately catalogs `நமது அனாதி மதம்` (1932), `நமது வேதங்களும் சாஸ்திரங்களும்` (1949), and `நாற்பது ஸம்ஸ்காரங்களும் கர்மானுஷ்டானங்களும்` (1950).

**Established:** five-part series existed by 1950.  
**Plausible only:** series is a direct topical reorganization/reprint of the 1932-33 publications.

## 1957–59 Madras chain

Official Kanchi biography: https://www.kamakoti.org/kamakoti/articles/SageOfKanchi9.html

It identifies three publication families from the 1957–59 discourses: `Acharya-svamigalin Upanyasangal` (three parts), *The Call of the Jagadguru* compiled by P. Sankaranarayanan (Ganesh & Co., 1958), and *Acharya's Call*. CiNii independently confirms the Kanchi 1958 book's compiler, publisher, place, date, and extent.

## Same-title guard

`The Call of the Jagadguru` is not a unique title. The wanted Kanchi work is Chandrasekharendra Saraswati / P. Sankaranarayanan / Ganesh & Co. / Madras / 1958. A distinct same-titled Sringeri work concerns Chandrasekhara Bharati. Never ingest by title alone.

## Pilot implications

- `அத்வைதம்`: official 1932 biography explicitly lists Advaita among discourse themes.
- `எளிய வாழ்வு`: official 1932 biography summarizes a call to clean, simple, unselfish, godly living.
- `சகல மதங்களுக்கும் பொதுவான பக்தி`: official biography records a 1931 Sriperumbudur teaching comparing paths to rivers reaching one ocean.
- `சிவ, விஷ்ணு அபேதம்`: official 1957–59 biography explicitly summarizes repeated teaching of Siva–Vishnu unity.

These are earlier thematic/doctrinal attestations. They are not yet proof that a DK chapter was textually derived from a specific earlier discourse.

## Arya Dharma

## Authority ladder

A. original tape / autograph / Sri Mukham  
B. contemporaneous report with item-level evidence of Acharya review  
C. contemporaneous reporter/notetaker account  
D. immediate print collection derived from contemporary reporting  
E. early edited/translated/reorganized collection  
F. retrospective biography attesting an earlier discourse  
G. Deivathin Kural attestation  
H. institutional digital transcription of DK  
I. derivative transcription/correction  
J. modern blog/social-media attribution

This hierarchy concerns evidence for historical wording and occasion, not spiritual authority.
