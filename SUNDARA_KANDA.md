# Sundara Kāṇḍa — Edition & Verification Contract

## Primary corpus lane
Primary target: the **critical-edition electronic text** represented by the GRETIL/Bombay-Indology 66-sarga Sundarakāṇḍa lineage.

Pinned GRETIL structure:
- Kāṇḍa: 5
- Sargas: 1–66
- native locus examples: `5.001.001a` … `5.066.029c`

## Critical recension warning
Many popular/digital Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa sites expose a **68-sarga Sundara Kāṇḍa**. Those are useful as traditional/vulgate witnesses but must **not** be silently merged into the 66-sarga critical-edition parent corpus.

Current discovery examples:
- Bombay Indology: electronic text of the critical edition, Book 5 Sundarakāṇḍa.
- SanskritDocuments mirror: exposes Sundarakāṇḍa through sarga 66; verification/reference candidate, but site reuse restrictions apply and textual independence from the GRETIL lineage must be established before it can satisfy the two-source rule.
- Several public reading sites expose 68 sargas; classify them as a different witness/recensional lane unless mapped explicitly.

## Verification policy
1. Never compare by sarga count alone.
2. Secondary witness must declare or be demonstrably compatible with the critical-edition 66-sarga structure.
3. Preserve source-native pāda markers and combine only matching a/b/c/d loci into a stable śloka record.
4. A 68-sarga witness may be used to document variants or traditional numbering, but not to certify critical-edition verse identity without an explicit alignment map.
5. Do not mark Sundara Kāṇḍa publishable until a genuinely independent critical-edition-compatible witness is pinned.

## Pinned constituted-text witness
Preferred transcription/locus witness: **The Vālmīki-Rāmāyaṇa, Critical Edition, Volume V: Sundarakāṇḍa**, edited by G. C. Jhala (Oriental Institute, Baroda).

This witness is deliberately classified as a **print witness of the same constituted critical edition**, not as an independent recension. Its role is to catch electronic transcription and locus errors in the GRETIL/Tokunaga-Smith lane. The later Goldman/Sutherland Goldman Volume V explicitly states that it is translated from the critical edition and is useful as a bibliographic/control witness, but its modern translation is copyrighted and is not corpus text.

Certification rule:
- GRETIL electronic text + Baroda constituted text agreement => transcription-certified critical-edition row.
- Agreement with a 68-sarga traditional witness is recorded separately and never used to renumber the 66-sarga parent lane.
