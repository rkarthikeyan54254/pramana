# Devī Māhātmya — Full Certification Contract

## Why this work matters to the moat
Devī Māhātmya is the first bounded Sanskrit work being pushed through the complete trust loop. The deliverable is **not** a generated devotional summary. The deliverable is a row-level evidence set where every authoritative record can answer: exact text, source locus, second witness, verification status, script normalization, and any unresolved variant.

**Moat test:** verified ground truth + provenance + refusal boundary = YES. Generated meanings/commentary are not authoritative corpus data.

## Canonical parent locus
- Parent: Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa
- Primary electronic witness: GRETIL, chapters **81–93**
- Native markers: `MarkP_81.1` … `MarkP_93.17`
- GRETIL notes warn that the legacy text was mass-converted and is **not proof-read**; this is precisely why second-source verification is mandatory.
- Exact transformed e-text licence: **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**.

## Independent verification lane
Secondary display witness: Ved Path, *Durga Saptashati*, chapters 1–13.

URL pattern:
`https://vedpath.org/en/durga-saptashati/adhyaya-{1..13}/`

Ved Path currently states © 2026 Ved Path, all rights reserved. We therefore use it as a **verification-only witness**. Do not copy its English meanings or commentary into the public corpus. Store only the evidence necessary to audit verification: secondary native locus, alignment score, review notes/hashes, and minimal excerpts where legally appropriate.

### Numbering is intentionally not forced
Verse numbers are not directly interchangeable. The secondary liturgical edition numbers standalone speaker/mantra cues (`uvāca`) that GRETIL often leaves unnumbered.

Example:
- GRETIL `MarkP_82.1` begins `devāsuramabhūd yuddham …`
- Ved Path chapter 2 numbers `ṛṣiruvāca` as 2.1, so the same substantive śloka is 2.2.

Verification therefore uses **ordered normalized-text alignment** while preserving both native loci. Sandhi word-boundary differences may be normalized for matching. A lexical difference is never silently normalized into agreement.

## Script normalization
GRETIL's source is romanized Sanskrit. The corpus target stores:
- `text_iast`: verified source romanization
- `text_original`: deterministic Devanagari generated by `scripts/iast_to_devanagari.py`

The converter is fail-closed. `tests/test_devi_script_spotcheck.py` compares representative converted strings against independently displayed Devanagari witnesses before promotion.

## Full promotion gates
A row may be marked `verified:true` only when:
1. its GRETIL locus is unique and within chapters 81–93;
2. normalized substantive text aligns to the secondary witness above threshold;
3. the secondary native locus is recorded;
4. no unresolved textual variant is hidden;
5. deterministic Devanagari conversion succeeds;
6. blocker flags (`needs_second_source`, `needs_script_normalization`, `needs_review`, `license_review`) are absent.

`audit_devi_certification.py` additionally requires 1:1 staging↔verification ID coverage and all 13 parent chapters.

## Product realization gate
On top of certified rows, `rag/grounded_synthesis.py` enforces claim-level support:
- every proposed claim must carry `support_ids`;
- every support ID must be a verified corpus row;
- direct quotes must be extractable from those supporting rows;
- unsupported claims are rejected rather than softened into model-memory prose;
- generated synthesis never enters the authoritative corpus.

This is the user-facing expression of the moat: **the answer is useful because each claim can be proven.**
